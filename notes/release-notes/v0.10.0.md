# v0.10.0 — Scoped rankings and research shadow predictions

## What changes publicly
The ranking page can now show sequential FBS, FCS, and conference ranks without changing the underlying combined Division I ranking. Conference rank follows national order, not conference record, and filtered views retain the combined D-I context.

The Accuracy page adds total-score MAE and displayed-tie rate. It also has a clearly separate hybrid shadow section once pre-kickoff research predictions have been collected.

## Research predictor
The new hybrid_core_v1 model is research-only. It combines:
- 40% of the existing monotonic rank-gap margin;
- 60% of a shrunk current-season offense/defense matchup margin;
- an offense/defense matchup total.

The initial offense/defense shrinkage uses three pseudo-games toward the current-season mean team score.

Research predictions are stored in research_prediction_snapshots rather than the official prediction_snapshots table. The worker locks only a research row that existed before kickoff, and research failures are isolated from the official prediction path.

## Score handling
Continuous research scores are retained for evaluation. If a context/model adjustment would produce a negative score, both teams are shifted upward equally until the lower score reaches zero, preserving the modeled margin. Integer display scores use winner-consistent rounding so a non-zero continuous margin cannot be shown as a tie.

## Context research foundation
The release includes shadow-only adjustment logic for:
- stadium demand grades A/B/C/D/F at +7/+5/+3/+1/+0;
- neutral-site stadium suppression;
- -7 per time-zone crossing, with capped variants available for testing;
- -7 for a meaningful altitude disadvantage;
- -7 to each offense for rain or snow.

Stadium grades are based on conference-relative current-season season-ticket sell-through inputs. Missing sell-through remains missing/neutral. Attendance and stadium capacity are not substitutes.

These context adjustments are not yet enabled in the worker. Venue/time-zone/elevation, ticket sell-through, and pregame forecast snapshots must first be populated with pre-kickoff provenance.

## Production safety
- Ranking model: division_i_weighted_v5, unchanged.
- Official predictor: rank_gap_v3, unchanged.
- Official Game Book history remains immutable.
- Context-aware winner flips are research-only.
