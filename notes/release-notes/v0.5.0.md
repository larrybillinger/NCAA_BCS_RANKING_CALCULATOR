# v0.5.0 — Neutral tied Week 1 baseline

Released 2026-09-17.

## Ranking change

Before Week 1, every NCAA Division I team is conceptually tied for first because no current-season evidence exists yet.

For scoring, the full tie uses the average rank of the positions occupied by the pool:

```text
Neutral Week 1 scoring rank = (N + 1) / 2
```

For the 2026 266-team pool, every in-pool Week 1 opponent therefore uses rank **133.5**.

After each completed week, teams with exactly the same season score share the average occupied rank for the next week's opponent scoring. Display order remains deterministic and separate from this mathematical scoring rank.

## Frozen-game rule

No retroactive rescoring was added. Once a game's points are earned using the ranking information available for that week, those points remain frozen.

## 2026 recalculation

The bundled Week 1 and Week 2 snapshots were rebuilt from the 2026 game data under the new rule.
