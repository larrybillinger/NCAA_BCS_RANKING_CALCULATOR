# v0.5.2 — Monotonic rank-gap predictor

Released 2026-09-17.

## Prediction rule

The projected winner now always follows the ranking order. If Team A is ranked higher than Team B, Team A is projected to win.

Projected margin is based directly on the numerical rank gap:

    projected_margin = points_per_rank × rank_gap

The current-season results calibrate the positive points-per-rank scale. The fit passes through the origin, blends toward a conservative early-season fallback, and is clamped so it cannot become negative or explode from a small sample.

Home field and a free intercept are no longer allowed to reverse the projected winner.

## Production identifier

    PREDICTOR_VERSION=rank_gap_v3

## Example

With Kansas State #40 and Tulane #105, the gap is 65 positions. At the fallback 0.20 points per rank, the expected margin is 13 points in favor of Kansas State. The live scale may move as current-season results accumulate, but the winner direction cannot reverse.
