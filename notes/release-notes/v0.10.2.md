# v0.10.2 — Game-aware live sync

Released 2026-10-08.

This release fixes live score updates. The ranking formula remains
`division_i_weighted_v5` and the official predictor remains `rank_gap_v3`.

## The problem

Up to v0.10.1 the worker polled CFBD hourly on Saturdays and once a day on every
other day. Weeknight games therefore did not update until the next daily poll,
and Saturday games still running after midnight Central dropped back to the
daily cadence, which could delay the final score and the weekly ranking by most
of a day. The sidebar used the same day-of-week rule, so it showed "CFBD OK"
while live games were roughly a day behind.

## New cadence

```text
Any Division I game in progress:  every SYNC_LIVE_MINUTES (default 60)
No game in progress:              until next kickoff + one live interval,
                                  at most SYNC_IDLE_MINUTES (default 1440)
Full schedule refresh:            at least every 24 hours (unchanged)
HTTP 429:                         existing exponential cooldown (unchanged)
```

A game is in progress from kickoff until it is final in the database, for at
most 8 hours after kickoff. The day of the week no longer matters.

Each live poll is one `/games` request. At the default 60 minutes a typical
week with a few weeknight games and a full Saturday uses on the order of
250–300 requests a month. Setting `SYNC_LIVE_MINUTES=30` roughly doubles the
live portion; check it against your CFBD plan before lowering it.

## Configuration

`SYNC_LIVE_MINUTES` and `SYNC_IDLE_MINUTES` replace `SYNC_SATURDAY_MINUTES` and
`SYNC_OTHER_DAYS_MINUTES`. The old names are still read if the new ones are
absent, and `synology-update.sh` copies existing values to the new names.

## Other fixes

- The sidebar marks data stale after 90 minutes while games are in progress.
- `/health` reports the number of live games.
- The conference dropdown follows the FBS/FCS selection, and "All" is
  highlighted only when no filter is active.
- The rank calculator validates ranks against the current pool size.
- The week dock lists every scheduled week and no longer shows a misleading "…".

## Production identifiers

```text
APP_VERSION=0.10.2
MODEL_VERSION=division_i_weighted_v5
PREDICTOR_VERSION=rank_gap_v3
```
